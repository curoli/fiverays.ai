# About Five Rays AI

**Empowering People to Make AI Work for Them.**

Five Rays AI is an independent software studio that explores and puts into practice new ways for people and artificial intelligence to work together. We develop software, try new technologies, and share what we learn.

## Our vision

**A world where more good ideas become reality.**

Many good ideas go unrealized because people lack time, money, technical skills, or help. AI offers new ways past those barriers.

We want creativity, curiosity, and imagination to play a bigger role in which ideas get made.

## Our mission

We use AI to expand what people can do and help others do the same. We see AI as a tool that supports human creativity, judgment, and responsibility while keeping people in charge.

## Our approach

Five related areas guide our work.

### 1. Create

We develop useful, innovative, and entertaining software. Our projects show what a small team with a limited budget can build.

### 2. Explore

We experiment with new technologies and ways for people and AI to work together. We study their capabilities, limitations, weaknesses, and risks. We value clear, reliable, maintainable software.

### 3. Empower

We build tools and methods that help people realize their ideas, make sense of information, and use technology on their own terms.

### 4. Share

We make our findings, experiences, and source code public where possible so others can benefit, build on our work, and explore their own ideas.

### 5. Include

We work to lower language, technical, and financial barriers by making software multilingual, understandable, and accessible where possible.

## The person behind Five Rays AI

Oliver Axel Rübenacker founded Five Rays AI and currently runs it on his own. The studio is itself an experiment in what one person can build with AI.

Oliver brings decades of experience in science and software development and a curiosity about what new technologies make possible.
