# Oliver Axel Rübenacker

*Founder of Five Rays AI, physicist, and software developer*

![Portrait of Oliver Axel Rübenacker](/assets/oliver.jpg)

## From curiosity to science

Oliver Rübenacker began programming at twelve and has been fascinated by the interplay of human thinking and computers ever since.

He was drawn to the natural sciences and to how mathematical models explain complex phenomena. He studied physics at the University of Karlsruhe and earned a doctorate in physics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, where he studied theoretical descriptions of quantum gases.

## From science to software development

His research took him from quantum physics to biophysics and computational biology. Software became central to his work, and he eventually became a software developer with a scientific background.

At the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard he developed software for biomedical research. Science, software development, and working with complex data shaped much of his career.

## A new chapter with artificial intelligence

In April 2025, Oliver saw that generative AI could write code within limits and began exploring its potential for software development. By early 2026, he was seeing coding agents under human guidance carry out entire software projects as well as individual tasks.

After more than two decades in the United States, Oliver returned to Germany in summer 2025. With Five Rays AI he began exploring how people and AI can work together in practice.

He is particularly interested in helping people realize ideas that once took too much time, money, or specialized expertise. People should set the direction and take responsibility for the result.

## Away from the computer

Oliver loves music and hiking and is especially fond of partner dancing. The combination of movement, music, creativity, and connection brings him joy.
