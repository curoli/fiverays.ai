# Privacy notice for offline apps


This notice applies to offline apps from Five Rays AI that link to it. It covers use of the app on your device.

## Data on your device

The app runs offline. It sends no data to Five Rays AI. Five Rays AI receives no usage analytics or crash reports from the app and has no access to data the app stores locally.

If the app saves settings or content, it does so on your device. You can remove local app data using the app or your device’s controls, where available. Exported files and backups can be managed separately.

## External services

If you download the app from an app store or use your device’s backup service, the provider of that service handles data under its own privacy notice. The same applies when you open an external website, including GitHub.
