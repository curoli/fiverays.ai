# TrayStack

TrayStack stores original contract files and processes their contents for search. It supports PDFs, JPEG and PNG images, and UTF-8 text and HTML. The original files remain available for download, and the extracted text records where it came from.

You can search the processed documents, review passages, and confirm or dismiss matches. The review workflow also distinguishes documents that still need checking from documents where the search found no active match.
